# scheduler — Prior art: the NFL formula

How the NFL builds its 17-game schedule (eight 4-team divisions; 17 games since 2021). Background for the athc schedulers — not the algorithm athc uses.

Source: [NFL Football Operations — Making the Schedule](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-schedule/making-the-schedule).

## The 17 games

- **6 divisional** — every division rival twice, home and away.
- **4 vs one same-conference division** — fixed rotation, whole division.
- **4 vs one other-conference division** — fixed rotation, whole division.
- **2 same-place games, same conference** — vs the same-place finishers in the two same-conference divisions not in this year's rotation.
- **1 same-place game, other conference (the 17th game)** — vs a same-place finisher in an other-conference division not in this year's rotation.

## The key property

**The 3 same-place games are the only games that differ between teams in the same division.** The other 14 are dictated by division membership alone. Those three slots are the NFL's entire strength-of-schedule adjustment: the division winner draws other winners, the last-place team draws other last-place teams.

## Notes

- The rotations guarantee every same-conference team is played at least once per 3 years, every other-conference team at least once per 4.

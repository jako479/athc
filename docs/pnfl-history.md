# PNFL — League History

What changed in the league from one season to the next: relocations
(a team renamed, same franchise) and division changes. The scheduler needs the
relocations as `[renames.<season>]` in `leagues\PNFL\scheduler.toml`; this
page is the record people read.

## Relocations

Previous name → new name, listed under the season the new name first plays.

| Season | Moves |
|---|---|
| 2045 | none |
| 2046 | Los Angeles Chargers → Buffalo Bills |
| 2047 | Indianapolis Colts → Baltimore Ravens · New York A Jets → Cincinnati Bengals · Houston Oilers → Miami Dolphins · New York N Giants → New York Giants (name only) |
| 2048 | Baltimore Ravens → Los Angeles Chargers |
| 2049 | Buffalo Bills → San Diego Chargers · Los Angeles Chargers → Baltimore Ravens · Seattle Seahawks → Detroit Lions |

## Division changes

From the league files (`E:\PNFL\League - League Files\<season>`).

| Season | Change |
|---|---|
| 2047 | AFC: Jacksonville and Miami (ex-Houston) moved West → East; Cincinnati (ex-New York A) and Baltimore (ex-Indianapolis) moved East → West |

## Playoff format

Four teams per conference: wild-card round, conference final, Super Bowl
(three rounds in the league file). Unchanged 2046-2048.
